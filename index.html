Structured execution across the project lifecycle

Frequently asked questions

Honest details regarding trial behavior, billing mechanics, data portability, and security.

When you initiate a Pro trial, your workspace unlocks all Pro features for 14 days without requiring credit card details. At the conclusion of the 14 days, you can choose to enter payment details to continue Pro, or your account automatically shifts to the Free plan with zero loss of task history or project records.
Yes. You can toggle your billing cadence directly in Workspace Billing Settings. When switching from monthly to annual, any unused portion of your current monthly cycle is prorated and credited immediately against your annual subscription invoice.
Your project data remains fully intact and exportable. If your workspace exceeds the Free tier allowance (such as having more than 3 active projects), surplus projects transition to read-only status until you archive projects or renew your subscription. We never delete customer data upon downgrade.
Nexora provides webhook endpoints and documented REST APIs. First-party integrations with GitHub, GitLab, and Slack allow bi-directional syncing of pull request statuses, issue branches, and milestone channel notifications.
Data in transit is secured with TLS 1.3, and data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 keys. Workspaces are logically isolated with row-level permission boundaries. Team plan subscribers can enforce SAML 2.0 Single Sign-On and download immutable audit logs for compliance audits.
Yes. You can invite external guests with scoped read-only or comment-only permissions to specific project boards without consuming paid team seats. Guests cannot browse private team workspaces or access other company initiatives.
